Claiming rewards

No action required for either validator. These are 0x01 validators, so the protocol automatically sweeps any balance above 32 ETH to the withdrawal address. That is why the withdrawal address already holds well above 32 ETH. There is no reward-claim transaction to send.

Irreversible. A processed full exit cannot be cancelled. After this, the 32 ETH is unstaked and swept to your withdrawal address (after the exit queue + ~27h delay). Rewards already arrive automatically; you only need this if you want to stop validating and withdraw the principal.

Verify the exit actually registered

The execution layer accepting your transaction is not proof the exit started — a malformed request is silently discarded by the consensus layer. Confirm here that the selected validator's consensus exit_epoch flips from FAR_FUTURE to a real number (it can take minutes to appear after inclusion).
